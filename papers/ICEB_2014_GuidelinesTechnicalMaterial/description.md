ICEB's international guidelines for producing mathematics, science, and computer notation in Unified English Braille, first released in 2008 and updated in August 2014. Its 17 sections define the compositional technical system: symbol identities, modes, recursive scope, layout, scripts, fractions, radicals, arrows, shapes, matrices, chemistry, and computer notation. Approved later revisions and errata override the corresponding base clauses.
