# Research Papers

- [Incremental Construction of Minimal Acyclic Finite-State Automata](Daciuk_2000_MinimalAcyclicAutomata/notes.md) - Daciuk, Mihov, Watson, and Watson's sorted incremental algorithm for constructing a minimal deterministic acyclic automaton without first materializing a complete trie.
- [Finite-State Transducers in Language and Speech Processing](Mohri_1997_FiniteStateTransducers/notes.md) - Mohri's sequential and subsequential transducer model, determinization boundary, and canonical minimization framework.
