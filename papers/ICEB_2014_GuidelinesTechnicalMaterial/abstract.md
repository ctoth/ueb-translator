## Our Interpretation

The Guidelines for Technical Material provide transcribers with rules and worked examples for representing mathematics, science, and computer notation in Unified English Braille. The document defines a finite symbol inventory and systematic composition rules for numeric and grade-1 modes, grouping, fractions, level changes, radicals, functions, modifiers, arrows, shapes, matrices, chemistry, and computer notation. Spatial structures are treated separately from linear expressions, and undefined print symbols require explicit transcriber definitions. The active standard must apply later ICEB-approved replacement sections and errata over this 2014 base.
