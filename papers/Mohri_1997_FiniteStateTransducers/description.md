Mohri develops the theory and algorithms of deterministic-input string and weighted transducers. The paper characterizes determinizable machines, proves input-linear application, and gives canonical minimization through output pushing followed by automaton minimization. For ueb-translator it establishes why a finite acyclic UEB rule dictionary can be exact, deterministic, and fast, while also delimiting the narrower final-output construction used by the compiler.
