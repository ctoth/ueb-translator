# Research Papers

## [Incremental Construction of Minimal Acyclic Finite-State Automata](Daciuk_2000_MinimalAcyclicAutomata/notes.md)  (finite-state, automata, dictionary-compression)
Daciuk, Mihov, Watson, and Watson's sorted incremental algorithm for constructing a minimal deterministic acyclic automaton without first materializing a complete trie.

## [Finite-State Transducers in Language and Speech Processing](Mohri_1997_FiniteStateTransducers/notes.md)  (finite-state, transducers, algorithms)
Mohri's sequential and subsequential transducer model, determinization boundary, and canonical minimization framework.

## [Guidelines for Technical Material, Section 3: Signs of Operation and Comparison](ICEB_2018_OperationComparison/notes.md)  (ueb, braille, technical-notation, mathematics)
ICEB's approved 2018 replacement for the technical-UEB rules governing mathematical operation and comparison signs. It defines the exact cells, semantic sign classes, spacing exceptions, minus handling, signed-number layout, hollow dot, and mathematical asterisk. It supplies normative rule-linked contracts for the translator's typed technical-notation API.

## [Unified English Braille: Guidelines for Technical Material](ICEB_2014_GuidelinesTechnicalMaterial/notes.md)  (ueb, braille, technical-notation, mathematics, science, computing)
ICEB's international guidelines for producing mathematics, science, and computer notation in Unified English Braille, updated in August 2014. Its 17 sections define the compositional technical system: symbol identities, modes, recursive scope, layout, scripts, fractions, radicals, arrows, shapes, matrices, chemistry, and computer notation. Approved later revisions and errata override corresponding base clauses.

## [Errata for Guidelines for Technical Material](ICEB_2014_GTMErrata/notes.md)  (ueb, braille, technical-notation, errata)
ICEB's 2014 errata for the earlier Guidelines for Technical Material. It corrects symbol mappings, grouping behavior, examples, references, and print/Braille distinctions across the technical code, and is applied as a precedence-aware overlay before implementation fixtures are derived.

## [Guidelines for Technical Material, Section 1.7: Choice and Placement of Grade 1 Indicators](ICEB_2025_Grade1Indicators/notes.md)  (ueb, braille, technical-notation, grade-1)
ICEB's July 2025 replacement rules for choosing grade-1 symbol, word, passage, and terminator scopes in technical expressions. It defines deterministic thresholds over symbols-sequences, special handling for embedded words, and explicit document-level policy alternatives.

## [Guidance on Transcribing Mathematics and Science in UEB](BANA_2026_MathScienceGuidance/notes.md)  (ueb, braille, technical-notation, bana, layout)
BANA's May 2026 United States and Canada supplement for mathematics and science transcription in UEB. It defines regional preferences for spacing, structural line breaks, continuation indicators, grade-1 scope, and spatial problems while retaining the ICEB rules as the international base.

## [The Rules of Unified English Braille, Third Edition 2024](ICEB_2024_RulesUnifiedEnglishBraille/notes.md)  (ueb, braille, contractions, backtranslation)
ICEB's official Third Edition 2024 rules for Unified English Braille. The scoped reading covers Section 10, which defines the contraction inventory, contextual legality rules, canonical preference order, shortforms, and expressly permitted transcription alternatives needed for a standards-based inverse translator.
