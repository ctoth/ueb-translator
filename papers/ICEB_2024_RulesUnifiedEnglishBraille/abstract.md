## Our Interpretation

The rulebook defines Unified English Braille as a context-sensitive notation with explicit modes, indicators, contraction classes, boundary conditions, and canonical preference rules. Section 10 makes contracted backtranslation necessarily set-valued: many cell sequences have rule-valid print expansions, and the standard itself permits alternative contractions when pronunciation or syllabification cannot be established. A conforming default decoder must therefore validate candidates against the same forward rules and expose unresolved ambiguity instead of applying an unstated dictionary or frequency preference.
